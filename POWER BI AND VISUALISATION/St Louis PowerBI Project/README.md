## Overview
This project was designed to answer a practical business question: when are customers most likely to shop on mobile devices versus desktop, and how can marketing teams use that insight to improve campaign timing and budget allocation? The analysis focuses on customer traffic patterns by device type throughout the day and translates those findings into a decision-support dashboard for business stakeholders.

The result is a Power BI dashboard that combines clear visual storytelling with actionable insight, allowing teams to evaluate digital engagement patterns and align marketing efforts with actual customer behavior.

## Business Problem
A marketing manager needed to determine whether customer activity shifted by device type across the day and whether marketing programs were being scheduled in the most effective windows. The core concern was that mobile traffic may peak during specific periods, but campaign strategy may not be aligned to those high-traffic moments.

This created an opportunity to answer a business-critical question:

- Are customers more active on mobile than desktop during specific times of the day?
- When do the strongest engagement periods occur?
- Should marketing budget and campaign timing be adjusted based on device behavior?

## Objective
The goal of this project was to transform raw website traffic data into a clear, executive-friendly dashboard that would help stakeholders:

- assess customer behavior by device type,
- identify time-of-day engagement patterns,
- support smarter campaign planning,
- and improve marketing decision-making with data-backed insight.

## Analytical Approach
To address this challenge, I developed a dashboard centered on a time-series comparison of mobile versus desktop activity across the day. The design focuses on the most relevant business question while keeping the visual simple enough for quick decision-making.

### Visuals & Insights
- Time-series trend lines comparing mobile and desktop traffic
- KPI cards highlighting core device behavior metrics
- Trend analysis to identify peak engagement windows
- Interactive filtering for exploring behavior by time period
- Clean presentation tailored for business stakeholders

## Why This Matters to the Business
The value of this analysis is not just in the chart itself, but in the business implication behind it. If mobile traffic consistently dominates during high-demand shopping windows, marketing teams can optimize campaign scheduling, promotions, and channel allocation around those periods.

This reduces wasted spend during slower periods and improves the likelihood of reaching customers when they are most engaged.

## Skills Demonstrated
- Power Query for data preparation and transformation
- DAX measures for business logic and KPI calculation
- Time-series analysis for trend evaluation
- Dashboard design for business storytelling
- Data visualization for executive communication

## Dashboard Insight
The dashboard clearly highlights when customer engagement is strongest and whether mobile usage is outpacing desktop during key periods. This makes it easier for stakeholders to validate the original hypothesis and respond with more informed marketing decisions.

## Conclusion
This project demonstrates how Power BI can turn a straightforward marketing question into a strategic business insight. By visualizing traffic patterns by device and time of day, the dashboard helps teams understand customer behavior more clearly and make more effective decisions around campaign timing, channel strategy, and promotional planning.

It is a strong example of how data visualization can move a business from assumption-based planning to evidence-based action.
