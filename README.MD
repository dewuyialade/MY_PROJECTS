## Overview
This project explores a practical marketing analytics question: whether customers are shopping more on mobile devices than desktops at specific times of the day. The dashboard was designed for **Cruz Adams**, a marketing manager who wanted to validate a key business assumption and adjust campaign timing based on real customer behavior.

## Business Challenge
Cruz believed that mobile shoppers were more active during certain periods of the day, but his marketing programs were not aligned to those patterns. The goal was to determine whether this hypothesis was valid and use the findings to improve promotional timing, audience targeting, and resource allocation.

### Key Questions
- Are mobile traffic levels higher than desktop traffic at specific times of day?
- Which periods show the strongest customer engagement?
- How should marketing campaigns be scheduled to match user behavior?

## Dashboard Story
The dashboard compares **device traffic trends over time** to reveal when customer activity peaks. By plotting **mobile vs. desktop** usage across the day, the report makes it easy to identify the hours where each device performs best.

This is especially useful for marketing teams because it helps answer:
- When should campaigns be launched?
- Should promotional creatives be mobile-first for certain time slots?
- Are there periods where desktop traffic is still dominant and should be treated differently?

## Visuals & Insights
The dashboard includes:

- **Time-series trend lines** comparing mobile and desktop traffic
- **KPI cards** highlighting core device behavior metrics
- **Summary comparisons** to show overall traffic patterns
- **Interactive filters** to support deeper analysis by time range and view

The strongest business insight is revealed when the mobile trend line rises above desktop traffic during rush periods. If the pattern is consistent, the marketing team can prioritize mobile-optimized content and campaigns during those windows.

## Why This Visualization Is Effective
A **line chart with time on the x-axis** is the best way to show this data because it makes behavioral changes over the day visible at a glance. It allows stakeholders to:

- detect spikes in traffic quickly,
- compare device performance directly,
- and decide where marketing investment should be concentrated.

This kind of dashboard turns raw website traffic into a strategic marketing decision support tool.

## Skills Demonstrated

- **Power Query** for cleaning and transforming the dataset
- **DAX Measures** for traffic comparisons and KPI logic
- **Time-Series Analysis** for identifying patterns across the day
- **Data Visualization Design** for a clear and professional business dashboard
- **Dashboard Storytelling** for translating raw numbers into business action

## Project Outcome
This project demonstrates how Power BI can be used to validate a real marketing hypothesis and support better campaign planning. Instead of relying on assumptions, the dashboard provides evidence-based insight into customer behavior and highlights the best opportunities for mobile-focused marketing strategies.

## Conclusion
The dashboard shows that even a simple behavioral question—whether customers shop more on mobile at certain times of the day—can become a powerful business insight when presented through the right Power BI visualization. It provides a clear, actionable way to understand customer behavior and optimize digital marketing performance.
