# Power BI Portfolio

This repository showcases a collection of business intelligence and data visualization projects focused on analytics, decision-making, and stakeholder reporting.

## Featured Projects

- [ALBERTA SCHOOLS PROJECT](POWER%20BI%20AND%20VISUALISATION/ALBERTA%20SCHOOLS%20PROJECT)
  - Education and public-sector analytics dashboard for school selection and regional comparison.

- [DATA JOBS PROJECT](POWER%20BI%20AND%20VISUALISATION/DATA%20JOBS%20PROJECT)
  - Data jobs market dashboard covering salary trends, job counts, and role comparisons.

- [St Louis PowerBI Project](POWER%20BI%20AND%20VISUALISATION/St%20Louis%20PowerBI%20Project)
  - Community and neighborhood analytics dashboard highlighting local trends and comparisons.

## Skills Demonstrated

- Power BI dashboard development
- Data cleaning and transformation with Power Query
- DAX measure creation and KPI design
- Business storytelling through visual analytics
- Data-driven reporting for stakeholder decision-making

## Project Goal

These projects reflect practical business intelligence work and are designed to demonstrate analytical thinking, dashboard design, and the ability to turn raw data into meaningful insights.
